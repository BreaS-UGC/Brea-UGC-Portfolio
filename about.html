About Brea UGC

With 15+ years of professional experience across acting, singing, and dance — including a specialty in commercial and comedic performance — Brea brings a performance background to UGC that most creators simply don't have. What started as a love for being on camera became a creative outlet that naturally merged her training with the world of digital advertising.

Beyond performing, Brea is a vocal coach of six years. That training directly translates to her content — natural pacing, clean delivery, and on-camera presence that holds attention.

Brea's approach combines direct-response marketing strategy with real on-camera skill. Her work covers concept ideation, scriptwriting, filming, retention editing, and creative strategy. Every deliverable is built with audience psychology and conversion in mind — content that's designed to perform, not just look good.

Her range covers lifestyle, pet, wellness, beauty, tech, and couple-oriented content, making her a solid fit for brands that need versatile output from a single creator.

Notable brand collaborations include MAELYS, Perplexity AI, Forbrain, Fresh Patch, EcoATM, and Too Good To Go — with multiple return clients speaking to the quality and consistency of her work.

Brea's goal is to build lasting brand partnerships around high-converting UGC, delivering content that pairs trained performance with strategic creative direction.
